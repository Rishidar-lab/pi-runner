# GROWTH — pi-runner

**Compiled:** 2026-09-27
**Scope:** Three growth surfaces kept strictly separate: (A) player scores in our game, (B) our app's position in the Pi ecosystem directory, (C) independent developer/campaign rankings. No invented ranking formula. No guaranteed top placement.

---

## A. Player Scores in Our Game

Player scores are an **in-game mechanic**. They have no monetary value unless we choose to redeem them (at our discretion). They do not affect Pi mining rates unless we build and disclose such a mechanism (and any mining-rate boost must comply with Pi app-usage boost rules — verify current requirements at developers.minepi.com).

**Principles:**
- Scores must be earned through genuine gameplay.
- No sale of high scores or leaderboard positions.
- No bots, no synthetic accounts, no fabricated engagement.
- Leaderboards (if implemented) must be clearly labelled as in-game and non-monetary.

## B. App Position in Pi Ecosystem Directory

### B1. Published Ranking Inputs

**None published.** The Pi Network has not released a public ranking formula for the Ecosystem Directory. Any claim about specific ranking inputs (downloads, ratings, engagement, staking volume) would be speculative.

**What is known from official sources:**
- Ecosystem Directory Staking "boosts ranking/visibility" (minepi.com/blog/pi2day2025/).
- No guaranteed top placement.
- No published weighting of factors.
- Per-category and per-user variation is not officially documented.

### B5. Growth Plan (App Visibility)

1. **Build enjoyable gameplay** — the primary driver of organic retention and word-of-mouth.
2. **Fast loading** — Pi Browser and mobile web environments have performance constraints; optimise asset size and load time.
3. **Clear metadata** — app name, description, icon, and category must accurately represent the game.
4. **Tamil/English localisation** — verified as an untapped differentiator in the Pi ecosystem.
5. **Voluntary feedback and sharing** — include optional sharing prompts; do not incentivise or fabricate reviews.
6. **Measure retention** — implement analytics to understand session length and return rate.
7. **Permitted community participation** — engage in Pi community channels transparently and honestly; follow all platform rules.
8. **Optional official directory support** — after separate approval, explore directory listing optimisation and staking visibility mechanisms.

## C. Independent Developer/Campaign Rankings

These are rankings maintained by third parties (e.g., PiNet verification status, community leaderboards, hackathon results). They are outside our control and should not be manipulated.

**Principles:**
- Do not fabricate reviews or ratings.
- Do not create fake accounts to boost rankings.
- Do not manipulate third-party leaderboards.
- Participate authentically in hackathons and community events.

---

## Explicit Exclusions (Not Permitted)

The following are **strictly excluded** from any growth strategy:

| Excluded tactic | Reason |
|-----------------|--------|
| Fake accounts | Violates Pi Platform rules; undermines trust |
| Synthetic engagement | Fraudulent; violates Developer ToS |
| Fabricated reviews | Deceptive; violates platform policies |
| Automated ad views/clicks | Ad fraud; violates Ad Network terms and possibly law |
| Third-party leaderboard manipulation | Unfair; violates community trust |
| Test users in production rankings | Pollutes data; misleading to players and directory |
| Buying or selling rankings | Not a legitimate growth strategy |
