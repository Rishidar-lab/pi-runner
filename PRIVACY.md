# Privacy Policy — Pi Runner

_Last updated: 2026-08-01. Replace the bracketed contact details before launch._

Pi Runner ("the Game", "we") is a browser game distributed in the Pi Network
ecosystem. This policy explains what we collect and why. We aim to collect as
little as possible.

## What we collect
- **Nothing, by default, to play.** Core gameplay runs entirely in your browser.
  Your progress (best score, π-token totals, settings, skins) is stored **locally
  on your device** (localStorage) and never leaves it unless you use a Pi feature
  below.
- **Pi account (only if you tap "Login with Pi").** Via the Pi SDK we receive
  your Pi **username** and a **user identifier (uid)**, and an access token we
  verify with Pi's servers. We do **not** receive your password, wallet keys, or
  personal contact details.
- **Leaderboard submissions (optional).** If enabled, a finished run sends your
  score, coins, distance, and (if signed in) your username to our server, which
  re-simulates the run to validate it. This is stored to display leaderboards.
- **Rewards / payments (only if you use them).** For the optional cosmetic
  purchase or play-to-earn rewards, we process the associated Pi payment/payout
  identifiers with Pi's Platform API. We do not store payment card or wallet
  secrets — those are handled by Pi.

## What we do NOT do
- No third-party analytics or trackers in the core game.
- No selling or sharing of personal data.
- No access to your device contacts, camera, or location.
- Advertising, if enabled, is served through the **Pi Ad Network** under Pi's
  own privacy terms; we only receive an ad identifier to verify a reward.

## Data retention & your choices
- Local data lives on your device; clearing site data removes it.
- Leaderboard/reward records tied to your uid can be removed on request
  ([contact: your-email@example.com]).

## Children
The Game is not directed to children under the age required by Pi Network's
terms in your jurisdiction.
