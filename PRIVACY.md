# Privacy Policy — Kurinji Run

_Last updated: 2026-09-27. Replace the bracketed contact details before launch._

Kurinji Run ("the Game", "we") is a browser game distributed in the Pi Network
ecosystem. This policy explains what we collect and why. We aim to collect as
little as possible.

## What we collect
- **Nothing, by default, to play.** Core gameplay runs entirely in your browser.
  Your progress (best score, π-token totals, settings, skins, language) is stored
  **locally on your device** (localStorage) and never leaves it unless you use a
  Pi feature below.
- **Pi account (only if you tap "Login with Pi").** Via the Pi SDK we receive
  your Pi **username** and a **user identifier (uid)**, and an access token we
  verify with Pi's servers. We do **not** receive your password, wallet keys, or
  personal contact details. The token is held only in memory for the session and
  is never logged.
- **Leaderboard submissions (optional).** If enabled, a finished run sends your
  score, coins, distance, and (if signed in) your username to our server, which
  re-simulates the run from its seed and input tape to validate it. Only
  server-verified runs are stored. The Node Challenge additionally uses a
  server-issued run session.
- **Payments (only if you use them).** For the optional cosmetic purchase we
  process the Pi payment identifier with Pi's Platform API to verify the amount,
  buyer, and network before approving. We do not store payment card or wallet
  secrets — those are handled by Pi. Rewards (A2U), if ever enabled, would record
  payout identifiers against your uid.
- **Ads (only if enabled).** Advertising, if enabled, is served through the **Pi
  Ad Network** under Pi's own privacy terms; we only receive an ad identifier to
  verify a reward.

## What we do NOT do
- No third-party analytics or trackers in the core game.
- No selling or sharing of personal data.
- No access to your device contacts, camera, or location.
- We never ask for your Pi passphrase or seed phrase. Enter those only inside the
  official Pi app.

## Data retention & your choices
- Local data lives on your device; clearing site data removes it.
- Leaderboard/payment records tied to your uid can be removed on request
  ([contact: your-email@example.com]).

## Children
The Game is not directed to children under the age required by Pi Network's
terms in your jurisdiction.
